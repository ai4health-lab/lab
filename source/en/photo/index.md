## Lab Introduction

The **Digital Life and Intelligent Health Laboratory** is located at the Institute of Fundamental and Frontier Research of the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). The primary research focus is on identifying causal biomarkers for human diseases and other complex traits. Guided by the **AI + Science** paradigm and aligned with the "Healthy China" national strategy, the team aims to explore the genetic mechanisms behind differences in disease susceptibility, providing theoretical support for the precise prediction of diseases in East Asian populations.

## Supervisor Introduction

**Xiaowei Mao**: Researcher and Ph.D. supervisor. His main research field is the analysis of causal biomarkers for complex traits. In recent years, he has focused on identifying causal biomarkers for human diseases and related traits. He has published SCI papers as the first author or corresponding author (including co-corresponding) in top academic journals such as *Cell*, *Science*, and *Science Bulletin*. Additionally, he has been selected for the Chinese Academy of Sciences’ Pioneering Talent Program and the Beijing Science and Technology Star Program (Medical Track).

His research achievements include:

- Unlocking the adaptation and selection processes of key genes for complex traits related to typical diseases in East Asian populations
- Systematically revealing the historical formation and adaptive trends of global population patterns
- Constructing animal disease models
- Developing computational biology methods related to life science big data

Projects led by him include:

- Outstanding Youth Science Fund from the Interdisciplinary Division of the National Natural Science Foundation
- Sichuan Provincial Science and Technology Activities Project for Returned Overseas Scholars
- Key Projects of the National Key Research and Development Program of the Ministry of Science and Technology
