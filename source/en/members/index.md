## Supervisor 

### Mao Xiaowei

**Position:** Professor, Researcher, PhD Supervisor, Head of the Big Data Life and Intelligent Health Laboratory at the University of Electronic Science and Technology  
**Research Areas:** Computational Biology, Statistical Genetics, Phenotyping, Molecular Epidemiology  
**Biography:**  
His main research area is the causal markers analysis of complex traits related to human diseases. He has been dedicated to interdisciplinary research combining biomedical sciences, informatics, and statistics to elucidate the genetic and evolutionary mechanisms of human disease-related complex traits. In recent years, he has published 9 important papers as the first or corresponding author in journals such as *Science* and *Cell*. He was selected for the Chinese Academy of Sciences Talent Program and the Beijing Science and Technology New Star Program (Medical Direction). During his master’s and doctoral studies, he received the EU "Erasmus Mundus World Program" funding twice to conduct research related to complex trait gene mapping, supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China’s interdisciplinary projects. He has led projects funded by the Ministry of Science and Technology’s National Key Research and Development Program, participated in significant projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation, and received the outstanding funding for technology activities from returned overseas students in Sichuan Province. He has been invited to present at the 5th International Quantitative Genetics Conference and the 11th World Congress of Genetics.
